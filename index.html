About Me

Physicist, Nuclear Engineer & Developer

I am a Nuclear Engineering M.S. candidate and Graduate Research Associate at The Ohio State University with multidisciplinary training in physics, software engineering, and computational nuclear materials.

My research focuses on radiation damage, defect kinetics, dislocation-loop evolution, nuclear fuels, numerical modeling, scientific computing, and the development of physics-based computational tools.

Projects

Research, modeling & software

Irradiation Defect Loop Modeling

Python · SciPy · Rate Theory · ODEs

Irradiation Defect Loop Modeling

Physics-based rate-theory modeling of irradiation-induced point defects and dislocation-loop evolution in uranium-based nuclear materials. The work combines stiff ODE systems, temperature-dependent defect kinetics, experimental TEM measurements, and nonlinear parameter estimation.

TRISO Pressure Modeling

OpenMC · Python · Depletion · Nuclear Fuels

TRISO Pressure Modeling

Computational modeling of internal pressure buildup in TRISO fuel particles caused by helium and fission-gas production. OpenMC depletion calculations are coupled with gas-inventory and thermodynamic calculations to study pressure evolution during irradiation and post-irradiation cooling.

2D Neutron Transport Solver

Python · Numerical Methods · Reactor Physics

2D Neutron Transport Solver

Development of a two-dimensional one-group neutron transport solver for reactor-physics calculations, including numerical discretization, transport boundary conditions, matrix construction, numerical solution, and verification.

Python · NumPy · SciPy · Pandas · Git

Scientific Computing & Data Analysis
